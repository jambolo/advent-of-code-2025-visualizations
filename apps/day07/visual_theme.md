# Visual Theme - Day 7: Laboratories

## Theme and Mood

The puzzle takes place in a **North Pole research laboratory** with a malfunctioning **teleporter**. The tachyon manifold has a distinctly sci-fi, quantum physics aesthetic. The "many-worlds interpretation" suggests parallel realities branching from a single origin.

**Mood**: Scientific, mysterious, high-tech, with an otherworldly glow suggesting quantum phenomena.

## Theme Object

### Palette
- **Background**: Deep space black (#0a0a12) with subtle grid lines
- **Tachyon beams**: Electric cyan (#00ffff) with bright glow effects
- **Splitters**: Warm amber/gold (#ffa500) representing the splitting mechanism
- **Timeline indicators**: Purple gradient (#9b59b6 to #8e44ad) for timeline counts
- **Grid lines**: Dim blue-gray (#2a3a4a) for the manifold structure
- **Source**: Bright yellow (#ffff00) pulsing glow
- **Text/UI**: Clean white (#ffffff) with cyan accents

### Shapes
- **Beams**: Vertical lines with gradient intensity, particle effects trailing
- **Splitters**: Chevron/arrow shapes (^) rendered as glowing prisms
- **Source**: Circular emitter with radiating rings
- **Timeline bubbles**: Small circles or badges showing timeline counts
- **Grid cells**: Subtle rectangular cells forming the manifold

### Motion Style
- **Beam travel**: Smooth downward animation with slight pulse
- **Splitting**: Explosive fork animation with particles scattering left/right
- **Timeline numbers**: Pop-in animation when counts increase
- **Merging**: Converging glow effect when timelines combine
- **Background**: Subtle floating particles suggesting quantum uncertainty

## Visualization Metaphor

**Quantum Beam Cascade**: The visualization depicts a particle accelerator or quantum experiment viewed from above. The tachyon beam is a stream of glowing energy descending through a crystalline manifold structure. Each splitter is a prism that fractures the beam into parallel realities.

Timeline counts appear as glowing orbs or badges that grow in intensity as numbers increase. The exponential growth is represented by increasingly bright and numerous particles.

## Rendering Approach

1. **Layer 1 - Background**: Dark grid with subtle glow at intersections
2. **Layer 2 - Manifold structure**: All splitters (^) rendered as amber prisms
3. **Layer 3 - Inactive beams**: Trail of where beams have traveled (dim cyan)
4. **Layer 4 - Active beams**: Current beam positions with full glow
5. **Layer 5 - Timeline indicators**: Floating numbers/badges above active beams
6. **Layer 6 - Effects**: Particle systems, glows, and split animations

## Motion and Emphasis

- **Split events**: Brief flash and particle burst, emphasizing the fork
- **Large timeline counts**: Larger, brighter indicators; maybe scientific notation for huge numbers
- **Final result**: Dramatic zoom-out showing all paths, then focus on total count with celebratory particle burst
- **Progress**: Current row highlighted with scanning line effect

## Storytelling

The animation tells the story of a single quantum particle exploring all possible paths through the manifold simultaneously. The viewer watches as one particle becomes many timelines, each fork multiplying possibilities. The final revelation of the massive timeline count creates a sense of awe at the exponential nature of quantum branching.
