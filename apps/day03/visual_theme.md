# Day 03 — Visual Theme

## Mood and story cues
- Setting: a grand art-deco lobby where escalators and elevators sit idle until fed with emergency batteries.
- Puzzle nouns (batteries, banks, escalator power) invite an electrical, mechanical vibe with polished metal and warm brass lighting.
- Motion should feel like current flowing through fixtures—snappy selections and glowing rails.

## Theme object
- Palette: midnight teal background, brass/gold highlights, jade and amber glows, coral accents for selections, soft ivory text.
- Shapes: pill-shaped “battery cells”, stepped escalator rails, beveled counters, thin neon lines to suggest circuitry.
- Motion style: sweeping light passes, slide-in trays, spark bursts when digits lock into place, escalator rails pulsing as charge accumulates.

## Metaphor and rendering
- Each bank is a row of battery cells embedded in the lobby floor. A scanning beam moves along the row, evaluating cells within a glowing window.
- Chosen digits rise into an overhead “power rail” that assembles the 12-digit joltage key; skipped digits briefly dim as the beam passes.
- The aggregate total is a central escalator spine: every completed bank sends a pulse up the rail, lighting additional steps toward the final destination.

## Influence on visuals
- Rendering emphasizes warm metallic highlights against a dark lobby backdrop; selections get coral flares and particle sparks.
- The scan window is a translucent jade band; the cursor is a brass-plated probe. The assembled digits sit in glassy capsules above the bank.
- Progress toward the final total lights more of the escalator with animated gradients, culminating in a bright activation of the top landing when complete.
